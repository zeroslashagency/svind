Same-Day Quoting Guide

Four figures for a fast crane quote

  1. 01 Safe Working Load (SWL in Tonnes) and whether an auxiliary hoist is required.
  2. 02 Bay span (rail centre-to-centre in metres) and total runway travel length.
  3. 03 Height of lift (hook floor-to-top clearance in metres) and headroom limits.
  4. 04 Duty class or shifts per day (IS 807 M3–M8; we survey on site if unsure).
