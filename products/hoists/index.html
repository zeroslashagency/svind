Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a wire rope hoist and a chain hoist?

Wire rope hoists use a multi-strand steel cable wound on a drum, suitable for higher capacities, longer lifts, and faster speeds. Chain hoists use a load chain and are typically used for lighter capacities and shorter lifts. Wire rope is standard for EOT and gantry cranes; chain is common for jib cranes and manual hoists.

Can I retrofit a new hoist to an existing crane?

Yes, provided the crane structure, runway, and electrical supply can support the new hoist. SVIND surveys the existing crane, checks girder deflection and wheel loads, and supplies a replacement hoist or complete crab unit that matches the duty class and span.

What spares should I stock for a wire rope hoist?

Wire rope, brake shoes or thruster brake linings, limit switches, rope drum, and motor brushes if contactor-controlled. SVIND manufactures rope drums, sheaves, forged hooks, and gearboxes in-house, so replacements are stocked at Harohalli.

What is S4 crane duty rating?

S4 is an intermittent duty rating per IEC 60034-1, meaning the motor starts and stops repeatedly with a 40% contact duration factor. Standard crane motors are rated S4, not continuous S1, because cranes lift in cycles rather than running continuously.

How often should wire rope be replaced?

Per IS 3177, wire rope is condemned when visible broken wires exceed the standard limits (typically 10% of total wires in one lay length), when diameter reduction exceeds 7%, or when rope shows severe corrosion or kinking. SVIND inspections include rope condition checks and written recommendations.

Do you supply hoists for cranes built by other manufacturers?

Yes. SVIND supplies replacement hoists and crab units for cranes built by other manufacturers, provided we can survey the existing installation and verify girder capacity, wheel loads, and electrical compatibility.
