Frequently asked questions

What does EOT stand for in EOT crane?

Electric Overhead Travelling. It describes a crane with an electric hoist that travels on a bridge, and the bridge itself travels along runway beams mounted overhead in the building structure.

What is the difference between single-girder and double-girder EOT cranes?

Single-girder cranes have the hoist suspended under one main beam, suitable for 1-15 T and light to moderate duty. Double-girder cranes have a crab unit riding on top of two beams, used for 5-100+ T, longer spans, and heavier duty cycles. Double girders give better hook approach and lift height but cost 20-40% more.

Which EOT crane type do I need for my factory?

It depends on capacity, span, duty cycle, and lifting frequency. For assembly and light manufacturing up to 15 T, single-girder is economical. For steel plants, foundries, heavy fabrication above 15 T, or when you need a cabin or auxiliary hoist, specify double-girder. SVIND surveys your bay and recommends the correct specification.

Do you provide installation and commissioning?

Yes. SVIND erects the crane, commissions it, and performs the on-site load test in front of your team. Erection and commissioning are quoted as separate line items so vendor comparisons stay honest.

What maintenance does an EOT crane need?

Periodic inspection per IS 3177: check girder deflection, rope condition and safety factor, dual-brake torque, gearbox oil, wheel and rail alignment, limit switches, and earthing. SVIND offers AMC contracts with scheduled visits and reports after every inspection.
