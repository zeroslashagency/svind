Configurations

0413

Six ways this crane gets built

The girder pair is the constant. What changes is the crab, the control position and the electrics - and each of those choices moves both the price and the maintenance burden.

The reference build: two welded box girders, top-running crab with wire-rope hoist, 2-speed contactor control and a trailing pendant on a festoon. Suits 5 - 32 T at M5 - M6 duty in a machine shop or fabrication bay where the operator works alongside the load.

Variable-frequency drives on hoist, cross-travel and long-travel, operated from a radio transmitter with a wired pendant kept as backup. Removes contactor burn-out as a failure mode and cuts load swing, which matters when the hook is carrying a die or a machined casting. Adds roughly ₹1 - 3 lakh per axis plus ₹50 k - 1.5 lakh for the remote.

A second, smaller hoist sized at 10 - 25% of the main SWL, for precise placement of lighter loads without cycling the main hook. Standard on press shops and power-plant turbine halls where one crane handles both a 50 T rotor and a 5 T cover plate.

Cross-travelling or fixed cabin with a maintenance walkway to IS 3177 platform requirements, under-bridge lighting and access ladder. Specified where the span is long, the bay is noisy or the operator needs line of sight into the load. Cabin adds about ₹2 - 5 lakh.

Deeper girders, larger drive selection, upgraded rope safety factor and redundant braking for 20-plus lifts an hour near rated load - cement mills, scrap yards, forging lines. The duty-class step alone typically adds 20 - 40% to the crane cost, and it is the single most common under-specification in a cheap quote.

Rail clamps and storm anchors, IP-rated panels, weather canopies over the crab, hot-dip or high-build epoxy paint system, and shrouded DSL instead of festoon. Also the base for dusty cement and corrosive chemical service. For molten metal the crane moves to the ladle specification with heat shielding and 8:1 rope factor.

Standard What it governs
IS 807 Design, erection and testing of structural steelwork - the permissible stresses, load combinations and deflection limits the girders and end carriages are calculated against. Read the explainer
IS 3177 The crane itself: duty classes M1-M8, wire-rope safety factors, brake torque, wheel-load limits, platform and access requirements, and the requirement definition sequence a buyer should specify against. RFQ checklist
FEM 9.511 Mechanism duty groups for the hoist, travel and traverse gear - how the drive train is sized for its expected load spectrum and running hours, cross-referenced to the ISO M-classes and CMAA classes A-F. Duty-class table

The six factors that move the number

  1. Span. Girder depth and plate thickness scale with span, and so does the wheel load your building has to carry.
  2. Duty class. Stepping from M5 to M7 typically adds 20-40%: heavier structure, larger drives, higher rope factor, better brakes.
  3. Hoist type and reeving. Main hook only, or main plus auxiliary; standard reeving or a true-vertical-lift arrangement.
  4. Power supply and controls. Contactor vs VFD per axis, festoon vs shrouded DSL, pendant vs radio vs cabin.
  5. Site conditions. Indoor, outdoor with storm anchors, dusty, corrosive or hot - each changes the paint system, ingress protection and electrics.
  6. Freight and erection. Distance from Harohalli, crane access at site, and whether your runway is ready when the crane arrives.

Questions

1113

Questions procurement engineers ask

When should I specify a double-girder EOT crane instead of a single girder?

Above roughly 15 T, above about 20 m span, or whenever the duty is severe or the crane must carry a cabin, walkway or auxiliary hoist. Below 15 T on light to moderate duty a single girder is 20-40% cheaper and is the correct engineering choice - SVIND will tell you so.

How is the duty class decided, and why does it change the price?

From a load-cycle calculation: lifts per hour, proportion of rated capacity, working hours. IS 3177 and FEM 9.511 map that into groups M3 - M8. A higher group means heavier structure, larger drives, higher rope safety factors and better brakes, so it can add 20-40% to the crane price. Under-specifying it is the most expensive mistake in a cheap quote.

What information do you need to quote accurately?

Safe working load, span between rail centres, runway length, lift height, available headroom, duty cycle, control preference, site power supply and the working environment. Those nine inputs are the IS 3177 requirement definition sequence, and they are the fields in the form below.

What is the maximum permissible girder deflection?

Vertical deflection under rated load is held to span / 750. That limit drives girder depth, plate thickness and stiffener spacing, and it is verified during the works load test before dispatch.

Why does a double girder give a better hook approach and hook lift?

The crab rides on top of the two girders rather than under a single beam, so the hook rises closer to the roof structure and travels nearer the runway centre line. In an existing building that recovers usable bay width and lift height you would otherwise have to build for.

Do I need VFD drives or are contactor controls enough?

For M5 duty and above, specify VFD: smoother acceleration reduces load swing and structural fatigue, and contactor burn-out disappears as a failure mode. Budget around ₹1 - 3 lakh per axis. On light-duty, low-cycle cranes contactor control is still defensible.

What does the quoted price usually exclude?

Runway girders and rails, civil foundation and gantry-beam work, the incoming feeder up to the crane isolator, freight and unloading, and GST. Load-test certification, erection and commissioning are quoted as separate lines so a vendor comparison stays honest.

Which spares should I hold in store?

Wire rope, brake shoes or thruster linings, limit switches, pendant cable and push-button station, and DSL collector shoes. Those are the items that stop a crane. SVIND manufactures the crab units, hooks, drums, sheaves, wheels and gearboxes behind them, so a replacement is a part number, not a search.
