Design and mounting requirements

Jib crane structural design

Pillar-mounted jib cranes require a concrete foundation designed for overturning moment and base shear. Wall-mounted jibs require adequate building column strength and embedment or through-bolting. SVIND provides anchor bolt drawings and loading calculations for your structural engineer.

Frequently asked questions

When should I use a jib crane instead of an EOT crane?

When the load moves in a small work area or workstation, and you do not need full bay coverage. Jib cranes are ideal for machine loading, assembly benches, maintenance bays, and toolroom service where the lifting is localized to one position.

What is the difference between pillar-mounted and wall-mounted jib cranes?

Pillar-mounted jib cranes are freestanding on a floor-mounted column, giving 360° rotation. Wall-mounted jibs are cantilevered from a building column or wall, typically giving 180-270° coverage. Choose pillar mount when you need full rotation, wall mount when the building structure can carry the load and you want to save floor space.

What is the maximum reach and capacity for a jib crane?

SVIND manufactures jib cranes from 0.5 to 5 T capacity with boom reach up to . Above 5 T or longer reach, an EOT crane or gantry becomes more economical and structurally sound.

Can jib cranes work outdoors?

Yes, when specified with weather-protected hoist, hot-dip galvanized or epoxy finish, and wind-rated design. Outdoor jib cranes are common in maintenance yards, construction sites, and loading docks.

How is the foundation designed for a pillar jib?

The foundation must resist the overturning moment from the rated load at maximum reach, plus base shear from slewing. SVIND provides anchor bolt layout and load calculations. Your civil engineer designs the foundation depth and reinforcement per soil bearing capacity.

What control options are available?

Pendant push-button for hoist up/down, or radio remote. Slewing is typically manual for light-duty jibs, motorized for higher capacity or frequent rotation. Hoist can be wire-rope or chain depending on capacity and duty cycle.
