Frequently asked questions

Do you supply spare parts for cranes built by other manufacturers?

Yes, SVIND supplies spare parts and components for cranes built by other manufacturers, provided we can identify the component specification from drawings, nameplates, or physical inspection. Common items like wire rope, brake shoes, wheels, and limit switches are standard across most manufacturers.

How quickly can I get a spare part?

[CLIENT TO CONFIRM — typical stocking policy and response time, e.g., fast-moving wear items delivered within 24 working hours to Bengaluru belt, fabricated components like rope drums within 3-5 working days]

Which spare parts should I stock for my crane?

Wire rope, brake shoes or thruster linings, limit switches, pendant cable and push-button station, DSL collector shoes, and fuses. These are the wear items that stop a crane. For critical production lines, also consider stocking a spare rope drum, gearbox seals, and motor contactors.

Do you provide installation for spare parts?

Yes, SVIND provides installation service for major components like rope drums, gearboxes, crab units, and DSL busbars. Smaller wear items like brake shoes, wire rope, and limit switches can be replaced by your maintenance team, or SVIND can do it under AMC scope.

How do I identify which spare part I need?

Best: send us the component nameplate or part number. Good: send photos of the failed part with dimensions. Acceptable: tell us the crane make, model, capacity, and what failed. SVIND can visit site to inspect and identify if needed.

Do you offer AMC contracts that include spares?

Yes, SVIND AMC contracts include scheduled inspections, maintenance labor, and consumables (grease, fasteners). Wear parts like wire rope, brake shoes, and limit switches are quoted separately when inspection finds they exceed safe limits. [CLIENT TO CONFIRM — AMC pricing structure.]

Preventive maintenance

0607

AMC prevents breakdowns

Scheduled inspection against IS 3177: girder deflection, rope condition and safety factor, dual-brake torque, gearbox oil, bearings, wheel and rail alignment, limit switches, and earthing. Worn parts are identified before they fail. Reports issued after every visit.
