Who we are

S.V. Material Handling System Private Limited designs, fabricates, installs and maintains overhead cranes at our own works in Harohalli KIADB Industrial Area, Bengaluru. The company was founded in 2006 and is managed by Shri D. Umapathi, Managing Director, with over 30 years of experience in material handling engineering.

We build single-girder EOT cranes for shop-floor duty, double-girder cranes to 100+ tonnes, and hot-metal and ladle cranes for foundries and steel plants. Every crane is engineered to the duty class of your bay and built to IS 807, IS 3177 and IS 4137.
