Why SVIND

Accountable, built in-house: why the drawing and the steel never drift apart.

SVIND started in 2006 with a plain engineering conviction: the people who calculate a crane’s girder camber and duty spectrum should be the same people who weld it, wire its drives, test it under proof load, and pick up the phone years later when a spare is needed.

Because we fabricate box girders, crab trolleys, rope drums, and end carriages in-house at Harohalli KIADB rather than brokering generic kits, replacement parts match your crane’s exact tolerances decades into its operating life.

No catalogue crane bent to fit. Purely accountable bay engineering.
